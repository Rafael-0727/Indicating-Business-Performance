Data visualization and Key Business questions answered

What are the monthly total sales?
The total sales for the business across all regions from May to July amount to approximately $1.04 million, with and average monthly sale of approximately $64.75 per order.

What is the total number of orders placed during this time period?
The total number of orders placed is of 16. This provides an insight into the volume of transactions that were processed.

What is the total marketing expenditure, and what is the monthly marketing expenditure?
The total amount of marketing expenditure is around $126,000 and per month respectively as total sum: May of $27,000 as total, June of $30,500 as total with an + $3,500 difference and July as total $68,200 with an + $37,700 difference.  

What is the change in sales over time for the sales teams, and how does this correlate with marketing spending and what is the performance of different sales regions with their advertising campaigns?
The waterfall chart indicates that the sales are increase as the amount of spending in marketing increases, thus we conclude that there is a positive correlation between Marketing Spending and Sales Revenue

What sales region had the highest sales during this time period, and how did their ranking change over time?
In the Ribbon chart we can see that the East Region consistently performed the best in terms of sales. However, even with substantial advertising spend, the West region did not perform proportionally well in sales, indicating potential issues with their advertising campaigns.
